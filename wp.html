02The problem: address poisoning问题：地址投毒

A wallet address is long: 42 characters on Ethereum-style chains, 34 on TRON. Most wallets shorten it to the first and last few characters with “…” in between. Address poisoning exploits exactly that.钱包地址很长：以太坊类 42 位，波场 34 位。大部分钱包只显示头几位和尾几位，中间用「…」。地址投毒钻的就是这个空子。

  1. You send money to someone you pay regularly.你给常付款的对象转一笔钱。
  2. Within minutes, a scammer sends you a tiny or zero-value transfer from an address with the same beginning and end. It now sits in your history.几分钟内，骗子用一个头尾一样的地址给你转一笔零头或 0 元。它就躺进了你的转账记录。
  3. Next time you copy the address from your history, you may copy theirs. A blockchain transfer cannot be reversed.下次你从记录里复制地址，复制到的可能是骗子那个。链上转账撤不回来。

03Three rules we do not bend三条底线

01

Zero private keys on our servers服务器上零私钥

No key that can move funds or sign on a blockchain is stored on our servers. Minting does not need one (section 4). When a design would need a server-side signing key, we pick a different design.服务器上不放任何一把能动钱、能在链上签名的钥匙。铸造本来就用不着（第 4 节）。哪个设计要在服务器上放签名钥匙，我们就换一个设计。

02

Only claims backed by evidence只说有证据的话

We mark addresses as “✅ verified”; we never call anyone a scammer on our own say-so. If we ever say an address imitates someone, the on-chain transactions that prove it come with the label, and so does a way to appeal. Order details stay private; each day we will publish a single fingerprint of all records, so history cannot be quietly rewritten (planned, section 8).我们只给地址亮「✅ 已认证」，从不凭一句话说谁是骗子。说一个地址在仿冒谁，一定附上证明它的链上交易，还给对方申诉的路。每一单的细节不公开；每天只公开一串由全部记录算出来的「总指纹」，谁也没法悄悄改历史（计划中，第 8 节）。

03

Safety warnings are never paywalled安全警告不收钱

If we see someone imitating your address, or a fake landing in your history, you are told — whether you pay or not. Paying buys convenience and scale: full details in every alert, more addresses, alerts in your group.发现有人仿你、或者你的记录里混进了假地址，不管你付没付钱，都会告诉你。收费买的是方便和规模：每条提醒都带细节、盯更多地址、发到群里。

0x000000000 will never ask you to connect your wallet, to sign or approve anything in your wallet app, or to send us money to “verify” something. We have no need to — so anyone who asks is not us.零九零永远不会让你连接钱包、不会让你在钱包 App 里给我们签名或授权、不会让你转钱给我们来「验证」。我们本来就不需要 —— 凡是这样要求你的，一定不是我们。

One thing the minting page does do: it signs a few short messages inside the page — when you order, collect and confirm receipt — with keys created on your computer for that order. They prove the order is yours. They are not transfers or approvals, and your wallet app is never involved.铸造页自己会做一件事：在页面里签几次名（下单、取货、交收条），用的是这一单在你电脑上造的钥匙。签的只是证明这一单是你的，不是转账、不是授权，也不经过你的钱包 App。

04How minting works: two halves, one owner铸造怎么做：两半钥匙，一个主人

A vanity address is an ordinary wallet address whose characters happen to form a pattern you chose, such as 0x0000000000…. Finding one takes billions of tries on a graphics card. The hard part is doing that work for you without ever learning your private key.靓号地址就是一个普通的钱包地址，只是它的字正好拼出你挑的图案，比如 0x0000000000…。要找到一个，显卡得试几十亿、上百亿次。难的是：替你把这些活干完，却始终不知道你的私钥。

1 · YOUR COMPUTER你的电脑
your half你那一半 🔒🔌 offline可断网

Your computer makes your half. It comes from your browser’s own cryptographic random-number generator and never leaves your computer.你的电脑造出你那一半。它来自你浏览器自带的密码学随机数，从不离开你的电脑。

2 · YOU → US你 → 我们
shadow影子→platform平台

It sends only the shadow of your half — a public key. A shadow cannot be turned back into the half: that one-way property is what protects every wallet.它只把你那一半的「影子」发给我们 —— 一个公钥。影子倒推不回那一半：保护每一个钱包的，就是这个单向性。

3 · OUR GRAPHICS CARDS我们的显卡
our half?我们那一半？✗ ✗ ✗ ✓

We search for the other half that makes the combined address match your pattern — billions of tries per second.我们去找另一半：让合起来的地址正好拼出你的图案 —— 每秒试几十亿次。

4 · YOUR COMPUTER你的电脑
yours你的+ours我们的=your key你的私钥 ✓

We send our half back. Your computer adds the two halves — offline — and gets a private key nobody else has ever held.我们把找到的那一半发回来。你的电脑把两半合起来（可以断网），得到一把谁都没拿到过的私钥。

What we hold: the shadow and our half. Without your half those two cannot make your key — and your half never leaves your computer.我们手里只有：影子，和我们那一半。没有你那一半，这两样合不出你的私钥 —— 而你那一半从不离开你的电脑。

This split-key method is not our invention: the Bitcoin Wiki describes it, and notes that the search can be handed to someone else without putting the final key at risk [24].这种「分成两半」的办法不是我们发明的：比特币 Wiki 就写了它，并说明把搜索交给别人做，最终的私钥也不会有危险 [24]。

Why it matters: in September 2022 a flaw in Profanity, a popular vanity-address generator, let attackers recompute private keys from addresses; the market maker Wintermute lost $160 million through a vanity admin address [22][23]. How a key is generated decides whether it is safe — here, the random part is created on your computer.为什么要紧：2022 年 9 月，常用的靓号生成软件 Profanity 被发现有漏洞，攻击者能从地址反推私钥；做市商 Wintermute 因为一个靓号管理员地址被盗 1.6 亿美元 [22][23]。私钥是怎么生成的，决定它安不安全 —— 在这里，随机的那一部分是在你电脑上生成的。

$Why does a scammer have to pay that much?$为什么骗子要花这么多？

Can’t a scammer just use the same method?骗子不能用我们的同一套吗？Yes. Anyone can rent graphics cards, and software for this is public. The tool is not what makes it hard — the number of characters to match is.能。显卡谁都租得到，这类软件网上也有公开的。难的不是工具，是要对上的位数。
So where does the difference come from?那差别从哪来？We need 10 zeros and any ending. He needs 10 zeros and the customer’s exact last 4. With our method he also gets a 10-zero address in about 3.4 minutes — but with a random ending, so he needs about 65,536 of them: 3.4 min × 65,536 ≈ 156 days.我们只要 10 个 0，结尾随便。他要 10 个 0，而且结尾正好是客户那 4 位。用我们的办法，他平均约 3.4 分钟也能拿到一个 10 个 0 的地址 —— 但结尾是随机的，平均要换 65,536 个才碰上：3.4 分钟 × 65,536 ≈ 156 天。
Why can’t he work backwards from the address?他不能从想要的地址倒推吗？An address can only be computed forwards from a key. If it could be reversed, anyone could steal any wallet. So everyone — us included — tries keys one by one.地址只能从钥匙往前算，不能倒推。能倒推的话，谁的钱包都能偷。所以谁都只能一个一个试，我们也一样。

What if he orders it from us?他来我们这里下单呢？It would take us the same 156 days, and we do not sell patterns that long. Before minting we will also refuse patterns that copy the ends of active on-chain addresses (planned).我们一样要算 156 天，而且这么长的图案我们不接。下单前我们还会拒掉「跟链上活跃地址头尾一样」的图案（计划中）。

07How the poison check judges an address免费查怎么判一个地址

It compares the whole address with everyone you have paid or been paid by. It looks first at behaviour, then at resemblance — a look-alike that has already planted something in your history is not “similar”, it is a poisoning address.拿整条地址跟你转过账的每一个对象比。先看它做过什么，再看它长得像不像 —— 已经往你记录里塞过东西的相似地址，不是「有点像」，是投毒地址。

1
Is it one of your counterparties, character for character?它跟你转过账的某个对象是不是一模一样？

Compare the whole address with everyone you have transacted with — not only the last one.拿整条地址跟你所有转过账的对象比，不只上一个。

identical → same address一模一样 → 同一个地址
2
Has it planted anything in your history?它往你的记录里塞过东西吗？

Dust, a zero-value transfer or a fake token to your address, from an address that resembles one of your counterparties (start: 3+ matching characters on TRON, 4+ on EVM, to be tuned).它往你的地址打过零头、0 元转账或假币，而且头尾跟你的某个转账对象有几分像（起步：波场 3 位以上、以太坊类 4 位以上，回测再定）。

⛔ poisoning address — with the transactions⛔ 投毒地址 —— 附上那几笔交易
3
How many characters match at the two ends?头尾合计有几位一样？

Count matching characters at the start and the end together, ignoring 0x and TRON’s leading T. Thresholds depend on the chain (below).把开头和结尾一样的位数加起来（不算 0x、不算波场开头的 T）。门槛按链定（见下）。

red: looks alike · yellow: somewhat alike红：长得像 · 黄：有点像
4
Show both addresses, aligned两条地址对齐摆出来

Matching characters green, differing ones red — you see at a glance that the ends agree and the middle does not.一样的位标绿、不一样的标红 —— 一眼看出「头尾一样、中间不一样」。

5
Three more checks顺手再查三样

Is it an exchange hot wallet (a deposit there may never reach your account)? A contract? Does its checksum hold (a typo breaks it)?是不是交易所的公共出金钱包（转进去多半进不了你的账）？是不是合约地址？校验码对不对（抄错一位就对不上）？

EVM — red from 7 matching characters, yellow at 5–6. By chance, a normal address reaches red against 500 counterparties about once in 70,000 checks.以太坊类 —— 合计 7 位以上标红，5~6 位标黄。拿一个正常地址跟 500 个对象比，碰巧标红约 7 万次里 1 次。
TRON — red from 5, yellow at 3–4. Each TRON character has 58 possible values instead of 16, so chance matches are rarer: about once in 220,000 checks.波场 —— 合计 5 位以上标红，3~4 位标黄。波场每一位有 58 种写法（以太坊类 16 种），碰巧像的更少：约 22 万次里 1 次。

These are starting thresholds. Before launch they will be back-tested on known poisoning addresses and ordinary ones: tightened if they cry wolf, loosened if they miss. Why not a fixed “first 4 + last 4”? MetaMask describes its check that way [16] — but in the TRON case of section 2 only the ending matched [6], and in the one TRM Labs analysed only two characters at each end [15]. Too loose is bad as well: EVM characters have only 16 values, so a person with 100 counterparties sees a random address share 2 end characters with one of them about 2 times in 3. Warnings that fire every day get ignored.这是起步门槛。上线前拿已知的投毒地址和正常地址回测：误报多了就收紧，漏了就放宽。为什么不死卡「头 4 尾 4」？MetaMask 写的判法就是这样 [16] —— 可第 2 节那宗波场案子只有结尾一样 [6]，TRM Labs 分析的那宗只对上两头各 2 位 [15]。门槛太松也不行：以太坊类每一位只有 16 种写法，一个转过 100 个对象的人，随便贴一个正常地址，约 2/3 的机会「头尾合计有 2 位一样」。天天响的警告，很快就没人看了。
