One key, two halves一把钥匙，劈成两半

~/0x000000000/idea.sh

You make one half on your computer. We mint the other half. The two halves only ever meet on your computer.你在自己的电脑上造一半，我们铸另一半。两半只会在你的电脑上碰面。

[1/6]Make your half — offline造你的那一半 —— 断网做

~/0x000000000/step-1.sh

Your browser rolls your half, s, and saves it as my-secret-s.json. Pull the plug first — an offline page cannot send anything.浏览器当场随机造出你的那一半 s，存成 my-secret-s.json。先断网 —— 断了网的页面什么都发不出去。

[2/6]Place the order — online下单 —— 联网

~/0x000000000/step-2.sh

Out go four things: the pattern, the chains, shadow A and a signature proving you hold s. The key itself never goes.发出去的只有四样：图案、链、影子 A、一个证明 s 在你手上的签名。钥匙本身永远不发。

[3/6]Pay in USDT用 USDT 付款

~/0x000000000/step-3.sh

Every order gets its own payment address, on TRON or BSC. Send the exact amount — the page watches the chain and moves on by itself.每一单都有自己专用的收款地址（TRON 或 BSC）。按金额转过去 —— 页面替你盯着链上，到了自己往下走。

[4/6]We mint — you watch it live我们铸造 —— 进度实时看得见

~/0x000000000/step-4.sh

GPUs try candidate after candidate — using only your shadow A — until one starts or ends with exactly your pattern.显卡拿着你的影子 A 一个一个地试，直到试出开头或结尾正好是你要的那串。

[5/6]Collect, go offline, build your key取货，断网，合成你的钥匙

~/0x000000000/step-5.sh

The other half, b, comes back. Then offline, your computer joins s + b into the full key, checks it is exactly your address, and locks it into a wallet file.另一半 b 送回来。然后断网：你的电脑把 s + b 合成完整私钥，当场核对就是你买的地址，再锁进钱包文件。

[6/6]Sign a receipt (optional)交个收条（可以不交）

~/0x000000000/step-6.sh

Sign one sentence with the full key. We cannot fake that signature — we never had your half. Skipping it does not affect your key.用完整钥匙给一句话签名。这个签名我们造不出来 —— 我们从来没有你的那一半。不交也不影响你已经拿到的钥匙。

Why we can't get your private key为什么我们拿不到你的私钥

~/0x000000000/why.sh

Shadow A is like a bank account number: anyone can see it, nobody can spend with it. From s to A is instant; from A back to s is a one-way street.影子 A 就像银行卡号：谁都能看，谁也拿它花不了钱。从 s 算 A 一眨眼；从 A 倒推 s 是一条单行道。
